# Screenshots

All 22 images are real screenshots of the running application. They were captured on 25 October 2026 by an automated Playwright script that drove the app in Chromium (1440 × 1000 viewport, 1.5× scale) and clicked through the pages in the order shown.

The data shown is 133 fictional **SAMPLE** jobs, 14 **DEMO CRAWL** jobs from the local fixture pages, and 8 **LIVE** jobs collected from the public Greenhouse/Lever boards of Groww and Paytm on 25 October 2026. The profile used is the built-in demo profile or the fictional sample resume.
